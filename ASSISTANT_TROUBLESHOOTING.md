# Companion follow-up fixes — 4.1.9

The S26 Ultra screenshot showed a successful Nano interaction and imported MAL counts, followed by incorrectly interpreted requests. Code inspection established three app defects: the model regenerated the complete constraint object for every turn, there was no minimum episode field, and model-generated mood labels were accepted without being present in the user's wording. These explain how a missing seed title and invented mood could reach the UI. No raw model trace from that phone session was available, so the precise output of the interpretation call is inferred from the displayed response.

## Corrections

- Interpret explicit edits, then merge them into app-owned state. Omitted/null model fields no longer erase a comparison, genre exclusions or length limits. The resolved seed MAL ID is retained, so follow-ups do not search for the seed again. A new comparison must be grounded in the user's wording; removing it requires an explicit request.
- Parse numeric episode limits in app code, including minimum/maximum, inclusive/exclusive comparisons and ranges. More than 26 means at least 27; under 24 means at most 23. A new length request replaces the previous interval. Invalid/conflicting/unrecognized explicit limits produce a clarification error while preserving the accepted state. Unknown/zero episode counts cannot satisfy a strict interval.
- Keep a bounded copy of the user's own preference wording for local comparison. Model-invented mood labels are ignored. Ambiguous ‘less/fewer kids’ prompts ask about child protagonists versus childish tone, with two reply buttons. The chosen comparison and limits remain visible above the composer. Previous matches are hidden during clarification, while their IDs remain available to interpret a reply.
- Mix seed links, MAL personal suggestions and favorite-linked candidates before the existing thirty-detail cap. A long seed list can no longer starve the other sources. Eight shortlisted candidates have slightly longer descriptions. The comparison step may return no confident match instead of being forced to choose irrelevant titles.
- Preserve ratings-based ranking, complete visible history import, content visibility, real-ID validation, watched/exclusion filters and local-only Nano inference. User wording stays in the in-memory conversation; it is not written into diagnostic reports.

## Evidence and limits

Regression tests reproduce the screenshot's sequence using a fictional catalog and scripted model. They also deliberately supply an interpretation that clears the seed, changes a format, invents a mood or attempts an incorrect exclusion. The app must retain authoritative state and enforce the requested length regardless of those outputs. Separate tests exercise comparator boundaries, bad ranges, unknown lengths, mixed age/rating numbers, pool fairness, empty selections and the clarification UI.

These checks establish deterministic app behavior, not the quality of real Nano recommendations. MAL's supplied descriptions do not reliably identify every protagonist's age or establish content warnings. Tone and cast requests remain approximate; they are passed to the local model in the user's wording and may yield no confident match. Candidate retrieval remains bounded rather than a search of the full MAL catalog. Real S26 Ultra inference and recommendation quality require another phone trial.

Phone trial: New chat → ‘I loved Made in Abyss. Find something similar.’ → ‘anything with less kids’ → choose ‘Fewer child protagonists’ → ‘more than 26 episodes’. Confirm that the comparison title remains, the visible limit says 27+ episodes, and every displayed result with a known length satisfies it. No match is an acceptable honest result when the candidate pool cannot satisfy the request. Then try under 24, an exclusion, and explicit removal of the comparison. A diagnostic report can confirm stages/counts without including conversation text.

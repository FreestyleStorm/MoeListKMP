# Illustrated profile assets

These original scenic assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool for the chosen expressive profile design. The existing C mockup supplied the style/mood reference; the first generated lake scene supplied the style reference for the city. No existing anime characters, franchise locations, logos, or licensed stills were used. The images are bundled with the app and require no image-service requests at runtime.

- `core/resources/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/profile_night_lake.png`: 1536 × 1024; header, watch-time hero, Completed and Plan to Watch.
- `core/resources/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/profile_evening_city.png`: 1536 × 1024; Watching.

Native Compose crops and overlays keep text readable in both themes. Decorative images have no accessibility label; the original category/statistic actions, press animations, and silent-refresh behavior remain. On Hold and Dropped use quiet tinted panels to keep the page from becoming visually crowded.

## Final generation prompts

### Night lake

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original decorative background for the header and illustrated watch-time/category cards of a native Android anime statistics app. The attached image is a STYLE AND MOOD REFERENCE ONLY; generate scenic art alone, no phone frame, no interface, no text or numbers. Original hand-painted anime background: tranquil Japanese mountain lake at blue hour/night, layered forested mountains, faint warm lights in a village across the lake, a crescent moon and a single delicate meteor in a richly detailed star-filled indigo sky, softly painted clouds and dark leafy silhouettes at the edges. Match the atmospheric cinematic anime-like art visible in the reference, with deep midnight teal, muted violet cloud edges and tiny warm glints. Wide landscape 1536x1024. Keep the left third and lower-left calm and dark for legible UI copy; the moon, distant lake and scenic detail concentrated in the right half. Good scenic composition when cropped to a wide horizontal banner and a rounded rectangular card. High quality painted illustration with gentle textured gradients, tasteful detail, not flat polygons. No characters, no recognizable franchise, no logos, no typography, no UI, no border, no watermark. Opaque image.

### Evening city

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original decorative background for the Watching tile in a native Android anime stats app. The reference image supplies the painted anime scenery style and restrained night palette ONLY. Create a matching original wide hand-painted anime background of an atmospheric Japanese city at blue hour: layered rooftops, a quiet railway and soft small warm window lights, a distant skyline, indigo-violet clouds and a few stars, softly glowing cyan reflections. No people or recognizable franchise locations. Leave the left third dark, calm and low-detail for a count and category label; put the scenic skyline and lights in the right two thirds, with strong interesting shapes that remain readable at a tiny card size. Cinematic painted anime illustration, rich but restrained, midnight blue and teal with subtle violet and warm amber accents, gentle brush texture, wide landscape 1536x1024. Matches the night lake reference as part of one visual family. No UI, text, logos, labels, borders, watermark. Opaque image.
